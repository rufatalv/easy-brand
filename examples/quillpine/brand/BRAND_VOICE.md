# Brand Voice: Quillpine

Untagged statements are **Observed**. A quick copy inventory is in [references/content-voice.md](references/content-voice.md).

## Principles

1. **Short and declarative.** Sentences average about 6 words. Imperatives come in threes: "Write once. Link everything. Find it again in a year…"
2. **Concrete over abstract.** The copy names real actions and objects: "drop a recording", "the sentence, the source and everyone who touched it", "a private spreadsheet of every vendor call". There is no "unlock", "supercharge" or "seamless".
3. **Notebook metaphors, lightly.** Words like "write", "margins", "notes", "field", "threads" and "§". The footer line "Written in many margins." shows the playful limit.
4. **Calm confidence.** No exclamation marks except one inconsistent CTA. No superlatives. Pricing is framed as relief: "No seat math."
5. **Benefits as outcomes over time.** "Find it again in a year." "Research that remembers itself."

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere except mono eyebrows, which are uppercased by CSS (the source text is sentence case).
- Headlines end with a period.
- Serial (Oxford) comma: not used ("Type, paste or drop").
- Plain numbers ("40-person", "5 hours"). Currency uses $ with no decimals.

## Headline construction

`[Subject] that [does something human].` For example: "Research that remembers itself." and "Notes that find each other."
Second pattern: `[Short fact]. [Short relief].` For example: "Two plans. No seat math."

## CTA patterns

- Verb first, 2–4 words, sentence case, no punctuation.
- Primary CTA: **"Start writing"**, optionally followed by `→`.
- Secondary CTA: "See how it works".
