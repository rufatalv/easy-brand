# Content Generation

## Voice

The voice is short, concrete and calm. Sentences average about 6 words. Name real actions and objects rather than abstractions. The notebook metaphor is used lightly.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere. Eyebrows are written in sentence case and uppercased by CSS.
- Headlines end with a period.
- No Oxford comma: "Type, paste or drop".
- No exclamation marks.
- Say "writer", not "user" or "seat".

## Headline formulas

| Formula | Examples |
|---|---|
| `[Thing] that [does something human].` | "Research that remembers itself." · "Notes that find each other." |
| `[Short fact]. [Short relief].` | "Two plans. No seat math." |
| `[Every X], [already Y].` `[Inferred]` | "Every interview, already linked." |

Limits:
- Headlines: 7 words or fewer.
- Ledes: 18 words or fewer; a triad of imperatives works well ("Write once. Link everything. Find it again…").

## CTAs

- Verb first, 2–4 words, sentence case, optional `→`.
- Default CTA: **"Start writing"**. Secondary: "See how it works".
- Never "Sign Up Now!", "Get started for free!!" or anything in title case.

## Vocabulary

| Use | Avoid `[Inferred from absence]` |
|---|---|
| write, note, link, find, recall, capture, thread, field, margins, context | unlock, supercharge, seamless, revolutionize, effortless, AI-powered, game-changing |

## Good and bad examples

| Good (from the site) | Bad |
|---|---|
| "Search returns the sentence, the source and everyone who touched it." | "Powerful AI search unlocks insights across your org!" |
| "Mention a person, theme or project and the note joins its thread." | "Seamlessly connect all your knowledge." |

## Quotes and claims

Attribute quotes by role and company type ("Head of Research, a 40-person design studio"). Never invent customer names, logos, metrics or testimonials. Use placeholders and say so.
