# Composition

## Always

- Left-aligned, with one focal headline.
- Hierarchy: § eyebrow → headline → body → action.
- Margins of about 7% of canvas width.
- Space from the 8px scale.

## Recipes

### Web section
- Hairline top border.
- 128px vertical padding (64px on mobile).
- 1200px container.
- Order: eyebrow, then 16px, then h2, then 48px, then the grid.

### Hero (web)
- 7:5 grid aligned to the bottom.
- Left: eyebrow, display, lede, then a primary ink pill and a ghost pill.
- Right: ruled note.

### Editorial social (1080×1350)
- Paper canvas with 80px padding.
- Eyebrow at the top, display below it, then a lede of at most 22ch.
- A hairline, then the logo bottom-left and the CTA pill bottom-right.

### Quote story (1080×1920)
- Forest canvas with a 250px top and 340px bottom safe area.
- Eyebrow, an italic Fraunces quote of at most 18ch, then a mono attribution.
- A ruled note pinned to the bottom of the safe area.

### OG (1200×630)
- Mirrors the hero: logo top-left, eyebrow and display bottom-left, ruled note right.

### Display ad
- Paper canvas with a 1px rule border.
- Eyebrow, display headline, then logo and CTA along the bottom.

## Card grid

- Cards: raised paper, 14px radius, 32px padding.
- Contents: mono numeral (`01`), a 48px gap, h3, then muted body.
- Layout: 3 columns from 768px, 24px gaps.
