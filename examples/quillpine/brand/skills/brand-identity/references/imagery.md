# Imagery

**Photography, illustration and screenshots are all Not established.** The site uses none of them.

## What to use instead

1. **A ruled note artifact.** Raised paper, 32px rule lines, an orange margin line at 48px, and a lift shadow. Inside, in order:
   - a mono meta line (`INTERVIEW 14 · 3 MAR`);
   - one or two sentences of specific, plausible research content, with one `<mark>`;
   - optionally an `→ linked to **Theme**` line;
   - forest tag chips (`#ops`).
2. **Type only.** For many formats the headline is the image.

## If imagery is unavoidable `[Recommended, not brand]`

- Product screenshots: put them in a raised-paper panel with a 1px rule border and a 14px radius. Don't use device frames or perspective tilts.
- Don't set text over an image. Keep text on paper or forest.
- Tell the user that photography direction is not established.
