# Brand Foundations

All values are observed in the Quillpine site unless tagged otherwise.

## Principles: do and don't

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Paper, not screen | Paper `#F4EFE6` canvas; raised `#FBF8F2` panels | Pure white, pure black, cool grays |
| Notebook motif | One ruled note with real-sounding research content | Ruled full-page backgrounds; more than one per view |
| Signal in small doses | § prefix, 1px margin line, `—` bullets, focus ring, highlighter | Orange buttons, cards, backgrounds or body text |
| Three voices of type | Fraunces headlines, Inter body, mono labels | Serif body, Inter headlines, mono paragraphs |
| Tight, heavy display | lh 0.95, -0.03em, weight 600 | All caps, loose tracking, light weights |
| One dark band | Forest for a quote or emphasis moment | Dark mode, multiple dark sections |
| Hairlines over shadows | 1px rule borders | Stacked or colored shadows, glows |
| Calm motion | Fade-rise once | Bounce, scale, parallax, loops |

## Logo

- Use only the existing SVG: a 28px ink tile with a quill stroke, plus the Fraunces "Quillpine" wordmark.
- Place it top-left in UI. On marketing canvases, place it bottom-left or top-left. `[Inferred]`
- **Not established:** a reversed (light-on-dark) version, clear space, and a minimum size.

## Known gaps

- Photography
- Dark mode
- Icons
- Forms and modals
- Error-message voice
- Positioning statement

Don't fill these gaps with invented rules. Ask, or mark the output as an extension.
